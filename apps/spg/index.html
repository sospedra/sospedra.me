How it works

Every renew fetches one random Wikipedia article. The password is a real sentence fragment from it, so it reads like language instead of line noise.

Length is what makes a password strong. Words make length cheap: a 28-character phrase is easy to remember and easy to type.

Everything is assembled in your browser. Wikipedia serves the article text and never sees a password.
