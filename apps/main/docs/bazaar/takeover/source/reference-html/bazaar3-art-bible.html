03

Design thesis

A hidden reclaimed night market built inside the decaying service infrastructure of an older facility.

Japanese back-alley intimacy, Blade Runner noir, CRT and lo-fi technology, improvised Fallout-like repairs, rusted industrial structure, warm decay, and playful inhabitants rendered with serious environmental logic.

It should feel

  • old but active;
  • illicit but inviting;
  • repaired rather than restored;
  • cluttered but graphically readable;
  • physically coherent while materially diverse.

It must not feel

  • like a mall or row of matching franchises;
  • like eight cards placed over wallpaper;
  • like one global cyberpunk tint;
  • like a fixed concept illustration that cannot respond to the viewport;
  • like random trash was used as a substitute for history.

Integration, never uniformity. Width, roofline, openness, material, sign, lettering, clutter, practical light, symmetry, and behavioral rhythm remain different.

04

Rendering, camera, and materials

Simplify rendering, never approved layout. Uses’ countertop angle is the camera Gospel.

Flat masses first

Large bounded colors, strong outlines, connected shadow shapes, sparse highlights.

Front-facing shallow oblique

Parallel projection, vertical uprights, horizontal counters, thin top bands, no convergence.

Quiet surfaces

Normally three tones per material. Detail collects only around identity and action.

Rendering contract

  • Genuinely authored at low resolution.
  • Strong near-black outer and structural outlines.
  • Chunky 16-bit-inspired clusters.
  • Three tones per material: shadow, body, highlight.
  • Nearest-neighbor enlargement only.
  • Readable at actual CSS size and around 300px preview width.
  • Dithering only as a deliberate local transition.

Camera law

  • Parallel projection, zero bay rotation, no vanishing point.
  • Posts, racks, walls, and character centerlines stay vertical.
  • Counter faces, beams, shelves, and floor seams stay horizontal.
  • Top planes are shallow bands, normally about 0.2 height-to-width.
  • No deep ellipse, trapezoid, side-wall wedge, or perspective correction.

Depth sequence

  1. Back wall.
  2. Rear infrastructure/recess.
  3. Rear stall structure.
  4. Character plane.
  5. Counter/display plane.
  6. Ground-contact objects.
  7. Foreground spill and shared lobby.

Standard new-asset grid

author:   320 × 421
enlarge:  exactly 3× nearest-neighbor
art:      960 × 1263
deliver:  960 × 1264 with one transparent padding row

Approved legacy families retain their existing dimensions. Grid and final-row policy remain manifest-specific.

Material shorthand

  • Metal: dark local shadow, desaturated body, tiny hard edge; rust only where caused.
  • Wood: warped/chipped masses; broad grain only; dark board gaps.
  • Concrete: large quiet planes; sparse cracks, damp paths, patches.
  • Fabric: broad flat folds, tension, faded pigment, torn edges.
  • Plastic: harder highlight; sticker ghosts and scratches; Games may be brighter.
  • Paper: cream/yellowed groups; no large pure-white fields.
  • Plants: deep yellow-biased greens in chunky overlapping masses.
  • Emission: one bright core and one or two hard receiver bands.

Reject the “classic AI” finish: painterly modeling, micro-scratches everywhere, random tubes and cables, antialiasing, soft glow, fine fronds, mixed pixel density, and detail invisible at display size.

06

Shared lighting grammar

Darkness alone does not integrate. Light must be visible, causal, material-aware, and cross the stall boundary.

01 · Source

A visible bulb, screen, hologram, lantern, lamp, or creature.

02 · Stall receiver

Hard palette-selected blocks on character, prop, and structure.

03 · World receiver

The same light touches nearby wall, beam, stair, trench, or floor.

04 · Response

Compact contact/cast shadow and occlusion consistent with that source.

Common value ladder

RoleApprox. L*Use
Outline / AO2–5Outer contour, deepest overlap
Deep shadow8–12Underside and cavity
Ambient shadow17–22Far/right plane
Material body29–36Front face
Top/key plane43–52Upward shallow band
Selected highlight58–68Focal edge, face, tool
Emissive core82–92Source pixels only

Plane law

  • Cool low-chroma ambient from upper/front-left.
  • Right/far faces one step darker; undersides two steps darker.
  • Front faces use material base; shallow top planes one step brighter.
  • Hard cast direction trends down-right at about 2:1, pending Uses calibration.
  • Background architecture remains one material step below stalls.
  • Lobby stays open; local light enters only the rear 8–12u.

Local light limits

  • At most three hard bands: core, direct receiver, weaker spill.
  • Core normally below 1% of stall pixels.
  • Colored receiver normally below 8%.
  • No blur, gradients, arbitrary alpha, CSS filter, color-mix(), or global tint.
  • Occlusion by signs, counters, racks, foliage, and bodies fragments every pool.
  • Hover changes source and declared receiver together by only 8–12%.

Current measured failure

The flatter mounted Console, Projects, and Travel assets contain effectively no pixels above luma 160. Their better source renders did contain bright UI, bulbs, and lanterns. Frequency-only quantization erased the sources. Emissive masks must be protected before palette normalization.

07

Physical integration system

Responsive modules, one open lobby, tenant consequences, and meaningful seam crossings replace the rejected fixed floor.

The stalls occupy the rear/background side. The lower/front part is a shared market lobby or walking aisle. Props must not be clipped at its lower edge.

Responsive environment kit

  • Tileable rear wall/environment bands.
  • Tileable ceiling, slab, fascia, and lobby-floor bands.
  • H-beams and columns at real load/seam points.
  • Flexible utility runs with authored junctions and ends.
  • Local rear/front tenant connection plates.
  • Unique contact, caster, and receiver plates.
  • Separate stair rear/core/front/light package.

H-beams are required structural ingredients, particularly at bay divisions and stair/floor junctions. They are not identical cages wrapped around every stall.

Meaningful seam crossing

  • Cable disappears behind equipment and returns in front.
  • Pipe visibly feeds a stall or drain.
  • Vine wraps shared infrastructure.
  • Rug passes under an exterior cable.
  • Rack bolts into a shared plate.
  • Platform wedges into the floor.
  • Light lands on both stall and architecture.
  • Tenant wear continues beyond the sprite boundary.

Grounding

Each stall receives exact support/contact islands, a compact caster tied to a visible source, and a tenant-specific floor response. No generic ellipse, common polygon, or shadow wedge.

09

Animation contract

The customer is the camera. The world stays registered; only deliberate anatomy, tools, screens, and effects move.

Exactly five states

  • Two idle frames.
  • Three hover frames.
  • Hover plays 1 → 2 → 3 once.
  • Frame 3 holds while hover/focus remains.

Absolute invariants

  • Same canvas, crop, transparent bounds, CSS box, scale, transform, and origin.
  • Nothing rescales.
  • No whole-layer translation.
  • Structure, sign, floor, counter, and fixed clutter remain byte-identical.
  • Character root remains fixed.
  • Torso never translates left or right.
  • No breathing by torso scaling.
  • Motion stays inside declared semantic masks.
  • Architecture and surrounding environment never change.

A character may articulate the head, face, arms, tool, or held object; extend; lean; bend; or bow around the fixed root. That freedom does not permit sliding.

Construction model

immutable rear plate
bounded character cel
bounded animated prop/effect cel
immutable front plate

A verified full-frame family is acceptable only when it is deterministically reconstructed from locked plates and masks. Never generate five unrelated complete scenes.

Verification harness

Machine PASS is evidence, not art approval. Both are required.

Sprite hard failures

Geometry

  • Canvas/crop/scale/transform mismatch.
  • Whole-frame shift or inset.
  • Broken 3× blocks.

Registration

  • Root or torso translation.
  • Sign/structure/fixed prop change.
  • Motion outside mask.

Pixels

  • Antialias/partial fringe.
  • Key spill or holes.
  • Palette explosion.

Identity

  • Normalization changes layout.
  • Character/stall redesign.
  • Motion clips envelope.
Integration and lighting hard failures

World

  • Pasted rectangle read.
  • Wrong rear/lobby staging.
  • Tile seam or overflow.

Ground

  • No real contact/caster.
  • Generic wedge/ellipse.
  • Clipped prop.

Light

  • No visible cause.
  • No world receiver.
  • Quantization deletes core.

Interaction

  • Decorative hit interception.
  • Architecture covers sign/action.
  • Floor/stairs change on hover.

Required evidence

  • Five-frame contact and onion sheets.
  • Motion heatmap and immutable byte diff.
  • Root/torso delta and motion-mask report.
  • Palette/grid/alpha report with protected emitters.
  • Source/receiver and contact/caster overlays.
  • Rear/mid/front layer breakdown.
  • Desktop/mobile scene captures and street diff.

Chrome matrix

  • Small phone, tall phone, 700px, and 701px.
  • 1023px, 1024px, and 1025px.
  • 1248px, 1440px, and 1728px desktop.
  • Pointer, keyboard, touch, reduced motion.
  • Every dialog, frame sequence, Up/Down target, and final-floor no-Down.
  • Mobile midpoint support and desktop stair aperture/landing/lane.
  • Unchanged street level.

Promotion gates

  1. Approved identity and distinctive construction remain intact.
  2. Uses camera is obeyed.
  3. Rendering is flat, outlined, chunky, and quiet.
  4. Palette swatches are exact and semantic.
  5. Every light has source, stall receiver, world receiver, and response.
  6. Unique contact islands and caster ground the stall.
  7. Architecture passes behind and in front.
  8. A meaningful seam crossing exists.
  9. Shared lobby remains readable and uncropped.
  10. Stairs belong to the same building.
  11. Responsive tiling works across the matrix.
  12. Structure, scale, root, and torso remain locked in animation.
  13. Dialog, signs, hitboxes, and scroll targets stay correct.
  14. Street diff is empty.
  15. The actual scene no longer reads as pasted sprites.
