4. GEOGRAPHY GAME DATA
================================================================================

Source revision geo-corpus-2026-07-27.2. The playable build reads only
committed, normalized files and derived assets. It contacts no source service at
runtime. URLs, checksums, and retrieval metadata live in
data/geo/sources.lock.json and data/geo/corpus-sources.lock.json.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Natural Earth

Country silhouettes and the unlabeled world map derive from Natural Earth 1:10m
vector data, pinned to repository commit
ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19.

  Terms:   https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
  License: public domain, per Natural Earth's own statement

  - ne_10m_admin_0_countries supplies a reviewed primary geometry feature for
    every country in the 194-country editorial roster.
  - ne_10m_land supplies the simplified, borderless world map.
  - The standard de facto Admin 0 worldview is used for every locale.

The derivative process is deterministic and documented in
scripts/geo/build-assets.mjs. It dissolves the display into path-only SVG,
projects each country with a country-centered sinusoidal projection, preserves
aspect ratio during its uniform fit, filters fragments below a fixed visual-area
threshold, uses deterministic line simplification, rejects active SVG content,
and names country shapes by their SHA-256 content hash.

Two reviewed geometry overrides keep silhouettes legible. France uses its
metropolitan European components. New Zealand omits remote antimeridian outliers
that would otherwise compress the two principal islands.

To reproduce the derived assets after placing the two pinned inputs under
work/raw-data/:

  node scripts/geo/build-assets.mjs \
    work/raw-data/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson \
    work/raw-data/ne_10m_land.geojson

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
flag-icons

Flag SVGs are copied from the 4x3 set in flag-icons version 7.5.0 for every
eligible ISO alpha-2 roster entry.

  Source:  https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons
  License: MIT, https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons/blob/v7.5.0/LICENSE

The original aspect ratio is preserved, SVGs are checked for active or remote
content, and output names use the first 20 hexadecimal characters of each full
SHA-256 digest.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unicode CLDR

English and Spanish territory names come from Unicode CLDR JSON release 48.2.1,
pinned to commit 26a79cb42bfcc90def764102aa2af126d9ef3108.

  License: Unicode License v3, https://www.unicode.org/license.txt

The committed country corpus vendors the exact published labels instead of
relying on the host runtime's Intl.DisplayNames data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wikidata

Country and capital entity IDs, bilingual capital labels, and capital
coordinates were normalized from a single Wikidata Query Service response
retrieved on 2026-07-27.

  License: CC0 1.0, https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing
  Query:   scripts/geo/wikidata-capitals.sparql

Both query and response digests are recorded in data/geo/sources.lock.json.
Wikidata is an import source, not final editorial authority.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GeoNames

The city corpus derives from the cities500.zip, alternateNamesV2.zip, and
countryInfo.txt snapshots dated 2026-07-27.

  License: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Readme:  https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt

Archive checksums and entries are recorded in
data/geo/corpus-sources.lock.json. GeoNames is an import source, not final
editorial authority. Corrections belong in
data/geo/editorial/city-overrides.json, with a reason and a stable GeoNames ID.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
World Bank

Country coverage uses the World Bank SP.POP.TOTL indicator for the pinned 2024
snapshot.

  Terms: https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/legal/terms-of-use-for-datasets

The exact API response is checksummed in data/geo/corpus-sources.lock.json. No
World Bank API call occurs at runtime.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Editorial scope

The versioned roster contains an editorially selected 192 United Nations member
states, the Holy See, and the State of Palestine. Territories and other
recognition-sensitive entities require an explicit roster revision. Eligibility
is an editorial gameplay decision, not a statement about recognition or legal
status.

To reproduce the committed country and city records from the pinned raw files:

  node scripts/geo/import-corpus.ts

5. CROSSWORD DATA
================================================================================

Retrieval details and checksums live in data/crosswords/sources.lock.json.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
USA Today crossword archive

Daily English editions replay puzzles from the USA Today crossword archive,
1995 to 2026, in xd format.

  Source:  https://xd.saul.pw/data
  Rights:  Copyright Universal Uclick / USA Today

scripts/crosswords/generate-replay.ts maps each site date to an archive puzzle
of the same weekday, newest first. Edition files carry render data only, meaning
the solution and the clues. The replay formula in the generator recovers each
edition's original archive date. The raw xd archive stays out of git and out of
the working tree. Re-download it from the URL above before regenerating. Served
editions live in content/crosswords/challenges/.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
spread the word(list)

The English fill corpus derives from spread the word(list), maintained by Brooke
Husic and collaborators.

  Source:  https://www.spreadthewordlist.com/
  License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
           https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

  - Used for candidate answers and their quality scores in the English fill
    corpus. That corpus and its build scripts are no longer in the repository.
  - Filtered to 3 to 13 letter A-Z entries scoring 50 or higher, minus a small
    banned list.
  - The derived corpus file inherits CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. This site is
    non-commercial and credits the list here.
  - Puzzles constructed with the list are the site's own, per the list's stated
    terms.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Spanish daily crossword

The Spanish daily crossword is a runtime feed. app/crosswords/spanish-daily.ts
calls the endpoint below and merges the result into that day's edition.

  Endpoint: https://backend.smartgames.media/api/game/crossword/last
  Rights:   retained by the publisher. Puzzle text is passed through, not
            redistributed as a corpus.

6. RADIO DIRECTORY DATA

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Radio Browser, used by /travel

The curated /travel radio snapshot uses station metadata from Radio Browser.

  Source: https://www.radio-browser.info/
  Rights: Radio Browser publishes its directory data in the public domain.

Stream audio remains owned and operated by the individual stations. This project
stores direct public stream URLs and does not redistribute their audio.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
mikbox74/boombox, used by /w98 RealPlayer

The RealPlayer station list started from the default corpus of mikbox74/boombox,
a retro boombox web player.

  Upstream file: src/data/stations.json
  Repository:    https://github.com/mikbox74/boombox
  Pinned commit: c629dede4ec7d0adf7af3bc69136790d6e521c69 (2022-11-10)
  Retrieved:     2026-07-28
  License:       none declared upstream

This snapshot takes only facts from it, meaning station names and public stream
endpoints. Every stream was probed independently on retrieval day. All display
names, taglines, badges, bitrate values, and verification stamps in
app/w98/realplayer/stations.json were authored for this repository from our own
probe headers, namely icy-name, icy-br, and content type.

Curation rules and runtime behavior are documented in
app/w98/realplayer/RADIO_DATA.md.
